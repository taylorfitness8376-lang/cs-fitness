🔗 1. Link Garmin Connect, Apple Health & Telegram
• Garmin Connect: Pulls daily Steps, Sleep & Workouts automatically.
• Apple Health (+ MyFitnessPal HealthKit): Unlike Garmin (which only gets total calories from MFP), MyFitnessPal → Apple Health syncs full macros (Calories, Protein g, Carbs g, Fat g, Fibre g, Water mL) + Steps, Sleep & Workouts!
• Manual Nutrition: Or type Calories, Protein & Water directly in Tab 1 Step 2.
Non-2FA & 2FA Supported
• Standard Garmin (No 2FA): Enter your Garmin Email & Password above and click 💾 Save & Connect (leave the 6-digit box blank).
• Garmin with 2FA enabled: Click 💾 Save & Connect first, then enter the 6-digit code Garmin sends you and click 🔐 Verify 2FA Code & Connect.
🍎 Apple Health + MyFitnessPal (iOS HealthKit Bridge) Full Macros + Steps/Sleep/Workouts
1. In MyFitnessPal (iPhone): Turn on More → Apps & Devices → Apple Health. MFP writes Calories, Protein (g), Carbs (g), Fat (g), Fibre (g) & Water (mL) directly into Apple Health.
