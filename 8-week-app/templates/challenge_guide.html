How to Use the 8-Week Challenge App (Challengers & Coaches)
🛡️ Coach Portal 🏋️ Open Challenge App

📲 Welcome to the CS Fitness 8-Week Challenge App

Your all-in-one daily tracker for Morning Weight, Steps, Macros, Water, Sleep, Strength Workouts, and Weekly Progress Photos. Save the app to your phone's Home Screen (`Add to Home Screen` in Safari/Chrome) for 1-tap daily access.

🌐 App URL: cs-fitness.onrender.com/challenge 🛡️ Coach Portal: cs-fitness.onrender.com/challenge/admin
1Getting Started & Signing In First Time Setup
  • Open the App: Visit /challenge and tap 🔑 Sign In / Switch User in the top bar.
  • Sign In with Your Email: Enter your email address and password. On your very first sign-in, the password you type will be saved as your account password.
  • Joining an 8-Week Block (New Challengers): Switch to the 🎟️ Join Block (Invite Code) tab in the Sign-In modal, enter the Block Invite Code from Coach Chris (e.g. CS-OCT2026), and enter your starting weight & body fat % — the app automatically calculates your daily Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fats, Water, and Weekly Step Minimums.
2Daily Routine — Tab 1: Morning Check-In (Takes 60 Seconds) Every Morning
  • ⚖️ Step 1 — Morning Weigh-In (Fasted): Weigh yourself first thing in the morning after using the toilet. Enter your weight in kg and tap 💾 Save Weight. The app tracks your 7-Day Rolling Average so normal daily water fluctuations never cause panic.
  • ⌚ Step 2 — Sync Garmin / Apple Health + Log Nutrition:
    • Tap ⌚ Sync Garmin (or use the Apple Health iOS Shortcut) to pull your Steps, Sleep, Resting HR, Body Battery, and Garmin Runs/Workouts automatically.
    • Enter your daily Calories, Protein (g), and Water (L) from MyFitnessPal / Yazio and tap 💾 Save Day.
    • Use the ◀ Verify Yesterday button anytime to finalize yesterday's full-day totals.
  • 🏋️ Today's Workouts & Deleting Mistaken Workouts: Log a CS Fitness class, Strength session, or Garmin run. Need to remove a duplicate or accidental workout synced from Garmin? Just tap the red 🗑️ Delete button next to any workout — it removes it and updates your day's training status (`GYM`, `CARDIO`, or `REST`) automatically.
  • 📸 Step 3 — Daily Scoreboard PNG: Tap 🖼️ Download Story PNG to save a clean summary card of your day for WhatsApp/Instagram.
3Strength Workouts — Tab 2: Workouts 1–4 3x Strength + 2–3x Cardio/Wk
  • Select Your Workout (1–4) & Week (1–8): Choose Men's or Ladies' Workouts 1–4.
  • Log Your Working Sets: Enter your weight & reps (e.g. 60kg x8, 8) and tap Save. Your previous weeks' badges (`W1`, `W2`, etc.) stay visible right beside each exercise so you can beat your numbers every week.
  • Automatic Roll-Up & Clear (`🗑️`): Saving sets automatically marks your day as GYM and calculates total weekly volume. Tap 🗑️ next to any exercise row to clear a set.
4Check-In & Photo Schedule — Tab 3: Weekly Check-In Important Schedule
  • 📸 Start Check-In — Day 1 MONDAY: On the very first Monday of the block, log your Starting Weight, Body Measurements, and upload your 3 Starting Photos (Side, Front, Back).
  • 🚫 Week 1 FRIDAY Check-In (NO Photos Required): Your first weekly check-in is on Friday of Week 1. Log your Friday weight and submit your Weekly Check-In write-up to Coach Chris & Laura — no photos are needed on the first Friday since you just took starting photos on Monday!
  • 📸 Weeks 2 to 8 — Every FRIDAY Check-In (+ 3 Photos): Every Friday from Week 2 through Week 8:
    1. Weigh in fasted on Friday morning.
    2. Upload your 3 Friday Progress Photos (Side, Front, Back — or use ✂️ Split 3-Pose Collage to slice a single 3-photo collage automatically).
    3. Write a quick note on how your week went and tap ✅ Submit Weekly Check-In to Coach (In-App).
  • 💬 Coach Feedback: Once Chris or Laura reviews your Friday check-in, their feedback and any macro adjustments appear directly inside Tab 3 of your app!
5Connecting Garmin & Backing Up Data — Tab 4: Settings & Sync One-Time Setup
  • ⌚ Connect Garmin Connect: Open Tab 4 (Settings & Sync), enter your Garmin email & password, and tap 💾 Save & Connect Accounts.
  • 📱 Quick Mode Switcher (Top Right): Prefer an ultra-simple mobile screen? Tap 📱 Daily 1-Pager or 📅 Weekly 1-Pager in the top header.
🛡️ Quick Guide for Coaches & Admins (Chris · Laura · Ewan) Coach Portal (/challenge/admin)
  • Accessing the Coach Portal: Sign in with your Admin email (Chris, Laura, or Ewan) or visit /challenge/admin.
  • 1. Cohort Summary Roster (~100+ Challengers): See everyone in one scannable table with clickable KPI filters (All Challengers, Needs Coach Review, Reviewed, Has Weekly Photos, Low Logging <3d). Click any challenger row to drill straight into their week.
  • 2. Review & Feedback: Read the challenger's Friday write-up, inspect their weekly averages & 3 photos, type your coach feedback, adjust Calories/Protein if needed, and click 💬 Publish Feedback to App.
  • 3. Side-by-Side Photo Comparison: Compare Start (Day 1 Monday) vs any Friday (Weeks 2–8) across all 3 poses (`Side`, `Front`, `Back`) with automatic scale weight delta.
  • 4. Manage Blocks & Users: Create sequential 8-week cohort blocks, onboard new challengers, or roll returning members into the next block (automatically sets their step minimum to 14,000/day).
