How to Use the 8-Week Challenge App (Challengers & Coaches)
🛡️ Coach Portal 🏋️ Open Challenge App

📲 Welcome to the CS Fitness 8-Week Challenge App

How the app works in 3 simple parts: 1) Set Up on Desktop once at the start → 2) Capture Daily on Mobile in 30 seconds each day → 3) Weekly Check-In on Desktop every Friday (Photos & Weekly Commentary).

🌐 App URL: cs-fitness.onrender.com/challenge 🛡️ Coach Portal: cs-fitness.onrender.com/challenge/admin
1Set Up & Initial Work on Desktop App Do Once on Laptop / Desktop
  • Sign In, Join Your 8-Week Block, or Reset Password: Open cs-fitness.onrender.com/challenge/login on your laptop/desktop:
    • New Challengers: Click 🎟️ Join with Block Invite, enter the Invite Code from Coach Chris (e.g. CS-OCT2026), your name, email, password, starting weight (kg), target weight, and body fat %. The app automatically calculates your daily Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fats, Water (L), and Weekly Step Minimums.
    • Existing Challengers: Sign in with your email and password.
    • Forgot Password? Click Forgot password? on the login screen (or open the 🔑 Forgot / Set Password tab) and verify using your Cohort Block Invite Code (CS-OCT2026) or a 6-digit Reset Code.
  • Link Garmin Connect (Works with or without 2FA) OR Apple Health:
    1. Click ⚙️ Settings in the top bar on Desktop to open Settings & Sync.
    2. Garmin Users (No 2FA): Enter your Garmin Connect Email & Password and click 💾 Save & Connect (leave the 6-digit 2FA box blank). You do not need to enable 2FA!
    3. Garmin Users with 2FA Enabled: Click 💾 Save & Connect first, then enter the 6-digit verification code Garmin sends you and click 🔐 Verify 2FA Code & Connect.
    4. Apple Watch / Apple Health Users: In the 🍎 Apple Health + MyFitnessPal box under Settings & Sync, click 🧪 Test Apple Health (+ MFP) Sync Now to verify the workflow, or click 📋 Copy My iOS Shortcut URL & JSON to copy your personal tokenized webhook URL (/api/challenge/sync/apple-health?token=...) for an iPhone Shortcut / Health Auto Export.
  • Day 1 Monday — Starting Weight & 3 Starting Photos:
    On the very first Monday (Day 1) of the block, open 📊 Weekly Check-In on Desktop (set to Start Check-In · Day 1 Mon), log your fasted starting weight, and upload your 3 Starting Photos (Side, Front, Back).
2Mobile Daily (Capturing Your Day-to-Day on Your Phone) 30 Seconds Every Day
  • Add to Your Phone Home Screen: Open cs-fitness.onrender.com/challenge on your phone (Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android) and tap Share → Add to Home Screen. When you sign on, the screen is cleared of everything else and opens straight into your Daily Update view (also available on the web app via 📱 Daily Update).
  • ⌚ Step 1 — Tap "Sync with Garmin": Tap the big blue ⌚ Sync with Garmin button at the top. It automatically pulls your daily Steps, Sleep, Morning Weight, and Garmin Workouts into the form.
  • 🔢 Step 2 — Check or Update Your Daily Numbers (Large Text): Review or type your numbers into the large, easy-to-tap boxes:
    • Weight (kg) (morning fasted scale weight) · Steps
    • Calories (kcal) · Protein (g) (from MyFitnessPal / Yazio)
    • Water (L) · Sleep (e.g. 8h 00m)
  • 🏋️ Step 3 — Tap Your Training + Add a Quick Note:
    • No need to log every individual gym exercise! Simply tap 🏋️ GYM, 🏃 CARDIO, 🔥 BOTH, or 🛋️ REST.
    • In the Workout / Training Note box right underneath, jot down a quick summary if you trained (for example: 3 x 5k at 5 min miles or Upper body strength + 20m incline walk).
  • ✅ Step 4 — Tap Submit & Auto-Collate:
    • Tap the big green ✅ Submit Monday button (shows the current day of the week).
    • You'll immediately get a confirmation banner saying "✅ Monday saved", and your day's stats are automatically collated into your 📊 Weekly Round-Up right below on the screen!
3Weekly Check-In on Desktop (Photos & Weekly Commentary) Every Friday on Desktop
  • Open Weekly Check-In on Desktop: Every Friday, open the app on your laptop/desktop and click 📊 Weekly Check-In in the top bar.
  • Review Your Auto-Collated Weekly Round-Up: All the daily numbers you submitted on mobile during the week are already collated into your weekly table and averages (Average Weight & % Loss, Average Steps, Average Calories, and Average Protein).
  • 📸 Check-In & Progress Photo Schedule (Monday Start → Friday Check-Ins):
    • Day 1 MONDAY (Start Check-In): Upload your 3 Starting Photos (Side, Front, Back) and starting weight.
    • Week 1 FRIDAY Check-In (🚫 NO Photos Required): Submit your Week 1 Friday weight and commentary — no photos are needed on the first Friday since you uploaded starting photos on Monday of that same week.
    • Weeks 2 to 8 — Every FRIDAY Check-In (+ 3 Photos): Upload your 3 Friday Progress Photos (Side, Front, Back — or click ✂️ Auto-Split 3-Pose Collage to split a single 3-pose photo automatically).
  • 📝 Add Your Weekly Commentary & Submit to Coach:
    • In the Your Weekly Write-Up / Reflection for Chris box, write how your week went (wins, energy levels, training notes, or questions).
    • Click ✅ Submit Weekly Check-In to Coach (In-App).
  • 💬 Read Coach Feedback In-App: Once Coach Chris or Laura reviews your Friday check-in, their feedback and any updated Calorie/Protein targets appear right inside your Weekly Check-In view.
🛡️ Quick Guide for Coaches & Admins (Chris · Laura · Ewan) Coach Portal (/challenge/admin)
  • Accessing the Coach Portal: Sign in with your Admin email (Chris, Laura, or Ewan) or visit /challenge/admin.
  • 1. Cohort Summary Roster (~100+ Challengers): See everyone in one scannable table with clickable KPI filters (All Challengers, Needs Coach Review, Reviewed, Has Weekly Photos, Low Logging <3d). Click any challenger row to drill straight into their week.
  • 2. Review & Feedback: Read the challenger's Friday write-up, inspect their weekly averages & 3 photos, type your coach feedback, adjust Calories/Protein if needed, and click 💬 Publish Feedback to App.
  • 3. Side-by-Side Photo Comparison: Compare Start (Day 1 Monday) vs any Friday (Weeks 2–8) across all 3 poses (Side, Front, Back) with automatic scale weight delta.
  • 4. Manage Blocks & Users: Create sequential 8-week cohort blocks, onboard new challengers, or roll returning members into the next block (automatically sets their step minimum to 14,000/day).
