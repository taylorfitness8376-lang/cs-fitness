The class board is separate from the existing gym TV. Update a class template, then tap Publish to TV. Saved edits stay private until you publish again.

The TV returns to standby when this duration ends. You can also clear it now.

Open TV board

If the TV is physically turned sideways but its browser still renders landscape, try either rotation option. Bookmark the matching address on the TV. Use the TV browser's fullscreen controls where available.

Long workouts rotate through numbered pages every 15 seconds rather than shrinking the text. Changes reach the TV within 10 seconds. If connection is lost, the last workout stays visible with a warning.

On your phone, use your browser's Add to Home Screen option for quick access to this planner. Samsung compatibility and physical mounting suitability still need checking on the actual model.
